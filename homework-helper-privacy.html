Homework Helper

Privacy Policy

Homework Helper is dedicated to providing students and educators with a secure, ad-free, and privacy-focused learning workspace.

1. Student Data Protection

We do not sell student data, show targeted ads, or build user profiling databases. Timetables, notes, task lists, and study schedules remain strictly on your local device unless cloud backup is manually enabled by the user.

2. Problem Solver & Academic AI

Math problems, assignment questions, or study queries submitted for step-by-step assistance are processed solely to return solution steps and learning guides. Submissions are anonymized and purged after rendering.

3. Storage & Storage Permissions

The application requests local storage permissions to allow exporting notes, saving timetable PDFs, and organizing study materials locally on your phone.

4. Contact Us

For questions regarding Homework Helper, reach out at kairolabs.official@gmail.com.
