NyayShield

Privacy Policy

NyayShield is engineered to safeguard your legal rights and emergency safety without compromising your privacy.

1. Offline Emergency SOS Data

Emergency contact lists, pre-configured SOS triggers, and location coordinates are stored locally on your device storage. Emergency alerts are dispatched directly via native cellular SMS/calls without routing through intermediate tracking servers.

2. Legal AI Assistance & RAG Queries

Legal informational queries sent to our AI legal assistant are processed anonymously. Case details, legal query texts, and uploaded document summaries are not sold, shared with third parties, or used to build commercial advertising profiles.

3. Required Permissions

  • Location Access: Used only when triggering an active SOS alert to attach coordinates to your emergency message.
  • Telephony / SMS: Enables one-tap call routing to verified helplines and emergency contact notification.

4. Legal Disclaimer

NyayShield provides legal informational tools and emergency routing, not formal attorney-client legal advice.
