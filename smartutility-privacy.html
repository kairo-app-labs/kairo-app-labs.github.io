SmartUtilityApp

Enterprise Privacy Policy

SmartUtilityApp provides B2B enterprise automation, multi-tenant authentication, and device management tools adhering to enterprise data isolation standards.

1. Enterprise Data Isolation & Authentication

User credentials and operational data are authenticated securely via token-based APIs (Firebase Auth / MySQL REST endpoints). Tenant data is logically isolated and encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3.

2. Telemetry & Real-Time Alerts

Operational alerts and push notifications are delivered exclusively for authorized administrative and system state notifications. Diagnostic logs are stored strictly for operational maintenance and troubleshooting.

3. Third-Party Sharing

SmartUtilityApp does not monetization, lease, or distribute operational logs or organization data to third-party brokers.

4. Contact & Support

For administrative or security questions, email kairolabs.official@gmail.com.
