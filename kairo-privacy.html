Kairo AI Assistant

Privacy Policy

At Kairo App Labs, we build with an Offline-First Edge AI architecture. Kairo AI processes your everyday voice commands, screen intelligence (Kairo Lens), and system actions directly on your device.

1. On-Device Data Processing

All primary AI inference, speech-to-text processing, and screen context analysis happen locally on your phone using our Edge Core pipeline. Your private inputs, voice audio, and screen data are never recorded, saved, or uploaded to external servers.

2. Hybrid Cloud Boost

When you explicitly request advanced web research or complex online operations, Kairo AI routes only the specific query to our secure cloud endpoint. No identifiable device telemetry or personal profiling data is attached to these requests.

3. Permissions & System Access

  • Microphone: Used exclusively for real-time local voice command recognition.
  • Screen Capture (Kairo Lens): Processed ephemerally in RAM for instant screen assistance and discarded immediately.
  • System Overlay / Accessibility: Used solely to trigger assistant actions from any app or lock screen.

4. Contact Us

For questions about Kairo AI privacy practices, contact us at kairolabs.official@gmail.com.
